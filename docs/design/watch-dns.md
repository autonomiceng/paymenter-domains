# Watch DNS observations (dormant)

Profiles hold an exact default NS set and separate website IP/CNAME and email MX/IP rules.
They default disabled; audited model edits require a transaction and the next revision.
There is no staff enable action, discovery, scheduled worker, customer surface or native resolver binding.
Later staff editing must use the existing watch permissions and native forms.

Above 100 sources, capture rotates one source plus apex using a durable source cursor; omitted hosts force partial points.
Internal composition can enroll at most two work rows per active sourced watch (delegation/points).
Capture locks current owner, native orders/services/products, mappings, profile, watch and sources.
Commit rechecks the same source/configuration stamp and exact claim after unlocked DNS work.
Changed ownership/property/profile, stale lease or a 45-second deadline discards the result.
Deadline/clock failures reject answer values, retain a failed-work marker for stale projection and back off.
A 120-second lease permits recovery without letting an expired worker overwrite its replacement.

Encrypted append-only observations carry owner/watch/source/profile/PSL identities and independent axes.
Unknown evidence never moves the last-good timestamp; projections retain the value explicitly stale.
Source/configuration changes and clock reversal also stale prior evidence immediately.
Delegation is normally six-hourly, hourly for fresh confirmed expected-platform DNS-only names;
points are daily. Transient backoff is 15 minutes, one hour, six hours, then daily plus <=10% jitter.
Local saturation without evidence defers 60 seconds without counting a resolver failure.
Completed partial rotation steps retain the normal cadence and never increment failure counts.
Routing digests ignore TTL and record order; encrypted facts retain those diagnostics.
Public-pool quotas, dispatch, confirmed drift/notices and 90-day retention cleanup belong to later slices.
Down/uninstall retain all data; missing migration history or partial retained tables refuse repair.

The pure classifier accepts complete RRsets, proven NODATA/NXDOMAIN or unknown independently.
A collector shares ten queries/45 seconds across apex MX and rotating primary-host CNAME/A/AAAA checks.
CNAME rules allow at most three links; loops and omitted/incomplete nonmatches remain unknown.
Known positives survive failures and mixed nonplatform targets remain explicit.
No SMTP/HTTP request or connection to answer addresses occurs.

Semantic label keys consume per-axis freshness: unknown, stale or partial evidence never emits
DNS only or Not pointed to company. Company text uses settings.company_name, trimmed/fallback us;
a future HTML presenter must escape it. There is no customer renderer in this slice.
Native empty answers are ambiguous and remain unknown; a DoH adapter for proven empty answers
is a follow-up. The classifier and fake-reader observer already exercise both proven-empty labels.

Ordinary native CNAME empties keep points partial; ambiguous_empty currently backs off and leaves them stale.
A later public health/circuit policy must distinguish that adapter limitation from resolver failure.
Native ambiguous empties also prevent confirmed empty labels and their hourly DNS-only cadence.

A source window advances only after its primary hostname finishes its permitted query path;
query-budget omissions retain the source while hostname priority rotates, preventing parity starvation.
