# Dormant acquisition send reservations

The genuine registration and transfer protocols may receive one account-bound
`WriteReservation`. The shared acquisition engine enters its operation scope,
reserves the exact mapped command, parameters and fresh USD term cost after
preflight, then seals the native funded attempt. NS3 consumes that reservation
immediately before the physical cURL attempt. It cannot change account, operation,
command, payload or UTC day, or authorize another send.

An exception while the exact grant remains unconsumed proves local non-dispatch.
The engine retains the original seal, claim, funding and counted reservation,
records a write-once typed unsent execution, and holds the operation. Refund proof
recognizes that exact acquisition result; it grants no automatic send retry.
An exception after consumption remains uncertain and cannot authorize a refund
or replay. Existing protocols without this optional reservation keep their behavior.

A reserved purchase never performs inline auto-renew removal. Its observed
correction remains authorization-required for the existing management producer.
Profile creation still needs a separate durable operation ledger and integration;
its present parent-claim checks do not supply that separate authority.

This is an internal acquisition boundary, not a complete runtime factory. No
writer is registered or activated for any account. Renewal/management reservation
hooks, profile and auto-renew operation assembly, trusted account/revision
admission and RuntimePorts composition belong to the next split. NS5 owns
activation. A11 needs synthetic installed Test-contract coverage here; the actual
connected acceptance run belongs to NS18. No provider calls are authorized.

The installed purchase regression uses genuine commerce, preflight, engine and
NS3 transport, with a separately injected synthetic profile port and namespaced
cURL responses. It cannot prove the remaining profile, factory or A11 contracts.
