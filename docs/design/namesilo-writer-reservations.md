# NameSilo renewal and management reservations

These are optional, explicitly injected engine seams. No factory, RuntimePorts entry,
request, worker or listener installs them. Acceptance/activation remains separate.

RenewalExecution repeats its existing funded admission and account guard, then enters
RenewalWriter's exact-account reservation scope. The exact renewDomain parameters and
fresh USD wholesale cost are reserved outside transactions before the durable funding
seal. The same parameter mapping reaches the transport. Only a live unconsumed grant
can turn a local transport exception into strict definitely-unsent execution evidence.
The existing delayed renewal replacement policy creates another sealed attempt on the
same paid debt; it never clears or reuses the original seal. Consumed/lost replies stay
uncertain and cannot authorize a refund or another write.

DomainManagement scopes one ManagementProtocol operation. After preparation, the exact
actual command (including unlock) and parameters reserve a zero-spend management call
before consume seals that operation. Admitted writes require a background context.
A live exact unconsumed grant can retain an immutable management_unsent marker through
the guarded ledger update. The original possibly_sent dispatch marker and seal remain.
That nonfinancial debt is safe for peer checks, but cannot replay; there is no new
replacement policy for a sealed management attempt. Unsealed work retains its existing
bounded recovery. A lost acknowledgement stays uncertain; email confirmation still
requires its original durable acknowledgement.

Auto-renew correction uses the existing CorrectionWork/ManagementCorrections authority:
a distinct nonfinancial operation linked to the exact current funded purchase/renewal,
with its own management grant, seal and fresh false readback. It cannot consume a
purchase grant, repeat a purchase, or create another correction after an unknown send.
No new routine staff step is introduced. Synthetic installed tests cover these seams;
writer assembly and A11 route composition remain later, connected acceptance is NS18.
