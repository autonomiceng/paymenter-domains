# Complimentary domain accounts

Staff with `admin.domains.fund` can set **Domains are complimentary** on the native customer editor. A reason is required. Extension-owned history retains every change, staff actor and timestamp. The native customer row is unchanged.

The setting applies to new registrations, transfers and renewal terms. Domain checkout lines become zero; hosting keeps its normal native charges. Each domain term retains its own account authorization and registrar cost ceiling. Turning the setting off restores standard pricing for new terms and leaves issued terms funded. The separate staff **Complimentary renewal** action still covers one renewal only.

Registrar price increases stop before spending. The domain page shows the quoted and usual cost in red. Staff with domain management and funding permissions can approve that exact quote with a reason. Approval retains the original claim and requires fresh registrar checks; it never bypasses premium, ownership, processing, prior-outcome or provider acceptance safeguards.

Each account-funded term has one native Paid $0 invoice, with no payment transaction or native Paid event. Until the registrar confirms the actual charge, its zero line says the cost awaits confirmation. Confirmation replaces it with the domain/action/term at the registrar charge and a matching negative **Complimentary** line. The two lines total zero and contribute no revenue. Native invoices display USD cents; the operation retains the exact provider charge. Receipt lines and Paid status cannot be changed through ordinary model writes, and receipts cannot accept payments. Repeated invoice or confirmation delivery does not create another term or discount.

Staff and customer domain pages label the account setting; the staff domain list filters by domain funding. Native offline regressions cover checkout, both registrar transfer paths, recurring renewals, switch-off, exact-cost approvals, receipt replay, customer/PDF rendering and native revenue queries. These synthetic checks do not establish live registrar or production database acceptance.
